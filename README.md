## The report

Checked 18 November 2026. The report compares the public Squarespace site with the staging build.

Squarespace publishes 12 sitemap URLs and 3 posts. It has no llms.txt. Its services page still uses the word metallic.

The staging build publishes 95 sitemap URLs and 60 guides. It has llms.txt, one homepage title and description, and a six-name service list: Residential and Commercial Floors, Surface Preparation, Concrete Repair, Levelling and Diamond Grinding, Crack Filling, Decorative Epoxy, and Anti-Slip Coating. Decorative epoxy on this site means custom flake blends. Guides for metallic, polished concrete, solid colour, polyurea, polyaspartic, hybrid systems, and shot blasting say those are not services on this site.

The report is not a ranking claim and does not include a new phone lab score.

## Updates

This delivery includes two small tweaks under [section 13](https://kr8tiv.io/terms/#revisions).

A small tweak is work that can reasonably be finished in about 30 minutes: a colour, a swapped line, one image, or another export. The two tweaks last six months from delivery of the final files. A new page, a new section, a full rewrite, or a layout change is a change order with its own quote.

The form is for Final Finish Epoxy only. It asks for the words on the site now, and the words that should replace them. Photos, short video, and PDFs can be attached. Up to 3 files, 12 MB each, 20 MB together.
