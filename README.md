## 🎨 What is this?
Welcome to **The Luminous Passage**! This isn't just a website; it's a jaw-dropping, high-end 3D architectural experience built for [Final Finish Epoxy](https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/). 

Instead of scrolling through a flat catalog of floors, visitors are transported into a serene, sunlit, classic-contemporary residence (think modern basilica vibes with beautiful arches and oculus lighting 🏛️). As you scroll, you physically *walk* through the space, watching the floors transform from tired concrete to stunning, decorative flake and metallic epoxy finishes.

## 🚀 The Tech Stack (Fast & Furious)
We're pushing the absolute limits of web performance and rendering here. No "AI Slop." Pure, agency-style craftsmanship.

* **Framework:** 🚀 Astro + React (Islands Architecture for insane SEO and LCP speeds).
* **3D Engine:** 🧊 Three.js & React Three Fiber (Off-main-thread rendering).
* **Motion & Physics:** 🌪️ GSAP (ScrollTrigger) for silky, cinematic camera dollys and liquid typography.
* **Models & Textures:** 🏔️ Blender 5.2 (Baked global illumination, KTX2/Basis texture compression, and Draco geometry compression).
* **CMS & Content:** 📝 Git-backed MDX with Keystatic (empowering Denis and his team to edit 50+ SEO blogs without touching code).
* **Visualizer:** 📸 Custom SAM (Segment Anything) floor-masking + WebGL compositing.

## 🛠️ The "Finish Alcove" Visualizer
One of the most ambitious features of this build is the **Finish Alcove**. Users can upload a photo of their own dirty garage. Our server-side masking adapter identifies the floor, and a perspective-aware WebGL shader maps one of **30 real epoxy finishes** flawlessly onto their space, complete with environment reflections and lighting adjustments.
