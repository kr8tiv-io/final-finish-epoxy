Final Finish Epoxy Coatings business card
Trim: 3.5 x 2 in
Bleed: 0.125 in on all sides (files are 3.75 x 2.25 in at 300 dpi)
Safe zone: keep text and logos 0.125 in inside the trim.
Masks: black = finish area, same size as the print file.
Stock: thick cotton or 32 pt.
Finish: painted edge (flake look, or the closest grey or black edge colour). The deboss look is printed in the art; a blind deboss of the logo is optional at a letterpress shop.
