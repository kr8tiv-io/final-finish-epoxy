# Metallic epoxy compared with a flake floor

The floors shown here are Torginol flake. Ask Denis what he will install on your slab.

Updated November 18, 2026

Metallic epoxy is not listed as a Final Finish service on this site. A sample photo is not a promise of a poured metallic floor.

Denis prepares the slab, fills cosmetic cracks, broadcasts Torginol flake into the wet base, and applies a clear coat. Anti-slip is a topcoat choice when the floor needs grip.

No schedule, price, or cure window is set on this page. Those come after Denis has seen the slab.
