# Garage Floor Coating in Edmonton | Final Finish Epoxy

This is a plain-text version of https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/services/garage-floor-coating-edmonton. The HTML page is the source of truth. The facts below match that page.

A Torginol flake floor for an Edmonton garage, built on Surface Preparation. A free estimate can start from photos or from a visit.

Before the estimate, it helps to know what is on the floor now: bare concrete, an old coating, or paint. Oil stains, de-icing residue, and any history of water are all worth mentioning. If the slab has in-floor heat, the site points to a guide on coating a heated garage floor.

Installation normally runs two days, and it varies with the concrete. A slab that needs more repair takes longer, and Denis will say so before he starts. Clear the floor before the crew arrives. Everything that lives in the garage has to come out, including shelving, tires, and a fridge in the corner. The bay is unusable while the work is on, and the dates are agreed rather than dropped on you.

Photographs of finished Edmonton garages are in the project archive, shot before preparation and after coating from the same position: https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/projects

Price, return-to-use time, and coverage terms are written into your quote, after the slab is seen. Every slab is different, and the estimate is free. The page says to be cautious of anyone who quotes price, timing, and coverage over the phone without seeing the concrete.

A coating makes the floor easier to clean, not self-cleaning.
