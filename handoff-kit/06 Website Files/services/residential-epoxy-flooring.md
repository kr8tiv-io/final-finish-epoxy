# Residential Epoxy Floors in Edmonton | Final Finish Epoxy

This is a plain-text version of https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/services/residential-epoxy-flooring. The HTML page is the source of truth. The facts below match that page.

Flake floors for barns, workshops, basements, kitchens, and bathrooms. A garage floor has its own page: https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/services/garage-floor-coating-edmonton

The finish is a flake blend chosen around the room. Final Finish installs Torginol polymer flake floors. Denis looks at the concrete first. A free estimate can start from photos or from a visit.

Interior rooms bring their own questions: an old coating under the lino, a patch where a wall used to be, a low spot that pools when you mop. Mention water history early. A coating will not stop water coming through a slab.

Installation usually runs two days, and it varies with the slab. An interior room also has to be emptied, and grinding is loud and dusty. A basement means carrying equipment down stairs, and a kitchen means the room is out of use while the work is on.

Price, return-to-use time, and coverage terms are written into your quote. A coating is not slip-proof, and the right texture depends on footwear, water, and how you clean. A coating also does not replace moisture work, ventilation, or heating.
