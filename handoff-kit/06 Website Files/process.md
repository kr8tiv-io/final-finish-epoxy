# How a Flake Floor Gets Built | Final Finish Edmonton

This is a plain-text version of https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/process. The HTML page is the source of truth. The facts below match that page.

Six steps, in this order, for a flake floor. Installation usually runs two days, and it varies with the concrete underneath.

1. A free estimate. Send photos of the floor, or book a visit. Either works, and both are free. Denis wants to see the concrete, what is on it now, and any cracks you already know about.
2. Grinding. The slab is ground to open the surface so the coating can bond. It is loud, dusty work, and the room has to be empty before it starts.
3. Cosmetic crack filling. Visible cracks are filled so they do not read through the finish. If something turns up that is structural rather than cosmetic, you hear about it then, not later.
4. The flake broadcast. Flake is broadcast into the coat while it is still open. This is where the colour and the pattern of the floor are set. Featured colours are at https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/finishes
5. The scrape. Once it has set, the surface is scraped back. That is what turns a loose broadcast into a flat floor you can walk on and clean.
6. The top coat. The last coat goes over everything. Where a room needs more grip, an Anti-Slip Coating goes on instead, and that is a decision made with you.

When you can park on the floor, and what it costs, depend on your slab and the conditions that week. Those details are written into your quote.
