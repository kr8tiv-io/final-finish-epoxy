# About Final Finish Epoxy | Flake Floors, Edmonton

This is a plain-text version of https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/about. The HTML page is the source of truth. The facts below match that page.

Denis runs Final Finish in Edmonton. Final Finish Epoxy Coatings was founded by Denis. The company does quality floor makeovers in Edmonton, Alberta. The floors are flake, and it uses Torginol products. The person quoting your floor is the person on it.

## How the work starts

Every job starts the same way. A free estimate comes from photos of the floor or from a visit, and the conversation is about the slab before it is about colour.

There is no showroom and no street address on this site, because the work happens at yours. Final Finish is a service-area business based in Edmonton.

## The order of work

Grind, fill, coat, finish. The process page at https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/process walks through the six steps.
