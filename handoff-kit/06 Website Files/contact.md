## What shapes a quote

The size of the floor, the condition of the concrete, any old coating, the repairs it needs, the finish you pick, and how equipment gets in. Photos are enough to begin. A wide shot of the room and a close one of any cracks tells Denis most of it.

## What happens after you send photos

Denis looks at the photos, asks about anything he cannot see, and either quotes from them or books a visit. Both are free.

The floors are flake, built with Torginol products. The process page is the order of work: https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/process
