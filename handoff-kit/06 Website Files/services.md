### Surface Preparation

Surface Preparation is the work that gets a concrete slab ready to hold a coating. The room is cleared, the condition of the floor is recorded, old coatings, oil and loose material come off, and the slab is diamond ground to open the surface. It is part of every Final Finish floor, and it decides how long the floor lasts.

- https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/services/concrete-solutions

### Concrete Repair, Levelling and Diamond Grinding

Diamond grinding opens the slab with diamond tooling so a coating can bond, and takes off what is already there. Concrete repair fills and rebuilds spalls and damaged areas, and levelling brings a floor back toward flat where low spots or elevation changes make it hard to use or to coat evenly. Larger repairs are quoted as their own line, with their own material and sequence.

- https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/services/concrete-solutions#concrete-repair-levelling-diamond-grinding

### Crack Filling

Crack Filling is part of every coating job. After the grind, visible cracks are filled so they do not read through the finish. A crack that keeps opening is a movement question, not a filler question, and Denis says so before work starts.

- https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/services/concrete-solutions#crack-filling

### Decorative Epoxy

Decorative Epoxy at Final Finish means a Torginol polymer flake floor: a base coat with flake broadcast into it, scraped back, then sealed under a clear top coat. Any Torginol flake colour is available, along with custom blends.

- Colours: https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/finishes

### Anti-Slip Coating

An anti-slip top coat is standard on every Final Finish floor. It is a textured last layer. On commercial floors the texture is balanced against how the floor is cleaned, because a surface the cleaning machine cannot clean trades one problem for another.

## The order of work

The finish is the last coat down, not the first decision. Preparation sets what the floor can be.

1. A free estimate. Send photos of the floor, or book a visit. Denis looks at the concrete first.
2. Grinding. The slab is ground to open the surface before anything is applied.
3. Cosmetic crack filling. Visible cracks are filled as part of preparation.
4. The coating. Flake is broadcast, then scraped, then a top coat goes over it.
5. An anti-slip top coat, standard on every floor. On commercial floors the texture is balanced against how the floor is cleaned.

Installation usually runs two days, and it varies with the concrete. More detail is on https://www.finalfinishepoxy.ca/process
