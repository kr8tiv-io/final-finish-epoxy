03 · Search

How the build is set up for search.

  • One clear homepage title and description. The description names Torginol flake, Edmonton, Surface Preparation, and the distance from the city.
  • Service pages have their own titles and descriptions: garage floors, residential floors, commercial floors, and concrete work.
  • Surface Preparation is in the menu and the footer, and it points at the concrete page.
  • The service list on the build is Residential and Commercial Floors, Surface Preparation, Concrete Repair, Levelling and Diamond Grinding, Crack Filling, Decorative Epoxy, and Anti-Slip Coating. Decorative epoxy on this site means custom flake blends. Anti-slip is a topcoat choice, not a separate trade.
  • Star-rating markup was removed. The site does not invent a review score.
  • Each guide is linked from the library and named in llms.txt, so a person and a crawler can find the same page.
  • Image descriptions were written for the pages they sit on, rather than one repeated line.
  • The name, phone (780) 991-1651, email finalfinishepoxy@gmail.com, and weekday hours match the public business listing used for the build.
  • The service area is Edmonton and Central Alberta, about 145 km out, including St. Albert and Athabasca. Twenty town pages are in the sitemap. They share one template. They are not twenty different stories, and photo locations labelled as a specific Edmonton garage or shop still need Denis Tadic to confirm.
  • Red Deer is not given its own town page. The service area stops where the 145 km note stops.
  • Canonical addresses point at www.finalfinishepoxy.ca so the review host does not compete once the domain is switched.

Phone lab score. The last recorded phone lab score on an earlier Hostinger copy of this project was 73, with LCP 2.5s and CLS 0. This 18 November review copy was not run through that test again. This report does not claim a rank, a traffic number, or a new lab score.

04 · GEO

How the build is set up for answer engines.

GEO here means giving a careful system a page it can quote without inventing the business. It is not a promise that a chatbot will recommend the company.

  • llms.txt states who the company is, what it installs, the six service names, the hours, and the Google listing.
  • Each guide has a short title a citation can use. Guides for systems the company does not sell say that in the title, then tell the reader to ask Denis Tadic what he will install.
  • The commercial page answers in plain language: the same flake floor for a shop, showroom, or corridor. Denis needs the traffic, the cleaning, and when the space can be empty. No schedule, price, or cure window is published before he has seen the slab.
  • The production robots file allows the public pages. The review host does the opposite, on purpose, so this staging address is not the one that gets indexed.
  • The Squarespace robots file does not publish an llms.txt and does not offer a short answer file for the 60 guides, because those guides are not on that site.

05 · Copy

What the writing will and will not say.

The build prepares the slab, fills cosmetic cracks, broadcasts Torginol flake into the wet base, and applies a clear coat. Garages, basements, shops, showrooms, corridors, and commercial floors are in scope.

A draft line about downtime as a written plan, and the planning paragraphs under it, were removed. They were not an accurate description of the work. The same pass shortened the guides that had drifted into systems Final Finish does not install on this site.

The site does not say the company refuses metallic epoxy. The live Squarespace services page still uses that word. Until Denis Tadic confirms the line, the build simply does not sell it.

The site does not invent a price, a cure time, a coverage term, or a portrait. "From this job" keeps the link on the word job, and that link goes to the company Facebook profile.
